# Project Brief: Thunderstorm Training App

**Mode:** Builder Mode
**Goal:** Having fun / side project — solving a real personal problem

---

## Problem Statement

Thunder sounds on YouTube are unpredictable in volume and timing, making it impossible to do controlled exposure training for thunder-phobic dogs. The randomness of YouTube videos (varying thunder clap volumes, unpredictable timing) frustrates the training process.

## Status Quo

- YouTube videos with random thunder sounds
- Unpredictable volume levels between claps
- No control over timing or intensity
- Frustrating for both owner and dog during training sessions

## Target User

- **Primary:** Dog owners with thunder-phobic dogs (starting with the project owner and their dog)
- **Secondary:** Dog trainers who can validate the method with their clients
- **Future:** Dog owners at large, once the app is polished

## Narrowest Wedge (MVP)

A web app with:
- **Big trigger button** — tap to play a thunder clap sound
- **Rain volume slider** — adjust ambient rain noise up/down
- Clean, simple, no bloat

## Key Answers

**What's the coolest version?**
- Multiple exposure sounds beyond thunder (car horns, New Year's Eve explosions, etc.)
- Training routines for other fear-based behaviors
- Session tracking to log progress over time
- Wearable dog tech integration (heart rate, stress monitoring) — very late stage

**Who would you show this to?**
- Your dog trainer — she can see how precise control enables effective training without YouTube's randomness
- Validation through a real professional user is a strong differentiator

**What existing thing is closest?**
- YouTube thunder videos (uncontrolled, frustrating)
- White noise machines (not specific to thunder training)
- No direct competitor with the one-tap trigger + rain control combo

**Fastest path to something usable:**
- Web-based MVP to develop and fine-tune to perfection
- Native mobile app for App Store distribution once solid

## Future-Fit Analysis

The vision grows from a simple thunder simulator into a comprehensive dog fear/anxiety training platform:
1. Multiple exposure sound categories (thunder, fireworks, traffic, etc.)
2. Built-in training routines for different fear types
3. Session tracking and progress logging
4. Wearable integration for physiological monitoring
5. Eventually: a native mobile app on App Store

## Premises

1. The unpredictability of YouTube thunder videos is a real barrier to effective exposure training for thunder-phobic dogs.
2. A simple web app with one-tap thunder triggers + rain volume control solves the core problem well enough to start.
3. Validating with yourself and your dog trainer first is the right path before building a native mobile app.

## Open Questions

- What other training routines could be done with this app beyond thunder exposure?
- What specific sounds would be most valuable for the exposure library?
- How should session tracking work — simple timestamps or more structured logging?
- What's the ideal audio quality and format for thunder sounds?
